Rules & terms

By participating in Frog and Friends or using Frog, you agree to follow these terms, the server rules, and Discord’s applicable policies.

Effective August 9, 2026

1. Respect and appropriate conduct

Treat members, staff, artists, and contributors respectfully. Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, bullying, personal attacks, explicit or disturbing content, disruptive spam, and pressure to share personal information are not permitted. Keep discussions civil and use channels for their intended purpose.

2. Honest community play

Trades, shops, advertisements, submissions, and agreements must accurately represent what is offered. Scamming, impersonation, misleading values or conditions, market manipulation, and real-money transactions involving Frog cards, tickets, currencies, accounts, or services are prohibited.

3. Fair use of Frog

Do not use alternate accounts to claim repeated rewards or bypass limits. Self-bots, macros, scripts, unauthorized automation, account access attempts, exploits, duplicated rewards, economy loopholes, and coordinated artificial activity are prohibited. Stop using a discovered bug and report it privately.

4. Virtual progress and transactions

Frog progress and virtual items exist for entertainment within Frog and have no guaranteed real-world monetary value. Review all trade, Shop, burn, sacrifice, and other permanent-action previews before confirming. Staff may not be able to reverse voluntary transactions.

5. Submissions and customization

Submitted artwork and customized content must follow current requirements and community rules. Do not claim another person’s work as your own. Submissions may be approved, rejected, returned for revision, or archived; approval does not guarantee immediate release, banner availability, or rarity.

6. Moderation

Depending on severity, frequency, and context, staff may warn members, remove content, restrict commands or features, issue timeouts, remove improperly obtained rewards, suspend access, or permanently remove accounts from the server. Severe violations may receive immediate action, and moderation evasion may affect alternate accounts.

7. Beta service

Frog is under active development. Features, rewards, prices, progression requirements, and content may change. Bugs, maintenance, resets, and temporary feature closures may occur. Availability or preservation of every beta feature or value is not guaranteed.

8. Account safety, privacy, and Discord

You are responsible for securing your Discord account. Never share your password, token, or recovery codes. Use of Frog is also subject to Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Frog’s handling of player information is described in the Privacy page.

9. Final note

No rulebook can name every harmful situation. Staff may act on behavior that clearly harms the community, its members, or Frog’s systems even when it is not described word-for-word here. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask staff before doing it.

This page is a plain-language community document and should be reviewed for the laws and operational details that apply to the project before a wider public launch.
