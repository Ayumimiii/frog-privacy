Privacy Policy

Your adventure belongs to you.

Frog only keeps the information needed to remember your journey, operate its Discord features, and protect the integrity of its game.

Scroll through the forest ↓

01

The Discord information Frog uses

Frog uses your Discord User ID to connect saved progress to the correct Discord account. This stable number is the primary key that identifies a player inside Frog.

Your current username, display name, and avatar may be accessed from Discord when Frog displays profiles, command results, trades, Shops, achievements, announcements, and similar social features.

02

When messages are processed

Frog processes message content when you invoke a prefix command or send text specifically requested by an interactive feature, such as a search, filter, name, or customization input.

The supplied text is used to understand and complete that action. Ordinary server conversation is not treated as gameplay progress.

03

Your saved journey

Frog stores gameplay records such as cards, inventory, currencies, progression, cooldowns, settings, customizations, Guild activity, Shops, trades, and relevant transaction histories.

These records allow Frog to preserve your progress, show requested features, complete transactions, prevent duplicate rewards, and maintain game integrity.

This information represents in-bot playtime progress—not a real-world identity profile.
04

Where information may appear

Information intentionally used in social features may be visible to other Discord members—for example, your Frog Profile, Showcase, public Shop, trade participation, Guild activity, or achievement announcements.

Private or anonymous display settings are respected wherever Frog provides them. Frog does not sell gameplay data or use it for advertising.

05

Information Frog never needs

Frog and the Frog and Friends Staff will never ask for your Discord password, account token, recovery codes, payment details, legal name, home address, or private contact information to provide gameplay.

Never share those details with anyone claiming they are required to restore cards, grant rewards, verify ownership, or provide support.

06

Retention and your choices

Gameplay records are retained while they remain necessary to operate Frog and preserve your account. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of data associated with your Discord User ID.

Some shared transaction records may need to be anonymized instead of simply removed so another player’s inventory and economy history remain accurate. Once anonymized, they will no longer identify your Discord account.

Questions or data requests

Talk to Frog and Friends Staff.

Contact staff through the official community server and include your Discord User ID so the correct records can be located.
