Inside Frog

Little systems,
one big journey.

Frog is built around collecting at your own pace. Every major progression system is optional, and cosmetics never make your cards stronger.

🌠

Wishes & Collection

Wish for characters across available banners, then organize cards with locks, tags, favorites, lists, and your Showcase.

🌌

Constellations

Sacrifice eligible duplicates for character EXP and unlock permanent Gallery, portrait, and Affinity Icon cosmetics.

💚

Affinity

Celebrate a favorite character through personal progression and supported Profile or Showcase customization.

🏪

Shop & Stockroom

Protect cards intended for sale in the Stockroom, list them at your chosen supported prices, or browse community listings.

🔁

Trading

Exchange eligible cards and items with other players through a confirmation-based flow designed for clear agreements.

🏕️

Guilds

Optionally join the Collector, Hoarder, or Ticket Master Guild and contribute to shared progression without pressure.

💎

Mythic Rarity

MR sits at the sparkling edge of Frog’s rarity garden. Its website preview uses animated color and shine without needing card art.

📖

Guides & Records

Learn each system through clear guides and review supported histories for trades, shops, and other important actions.

🧪

Growing Beta

Frog is actively developed. Features and values may change, with responsible feedback and bug reports always welcome.
