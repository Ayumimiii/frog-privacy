Frog field notes

own pace.

Short, player-friendly explanations of Frog’s larger systems. The bot’s current previews and confirmation screens remain the source for exact live values.

🌌

Constellations

Use fconstellation to sacrifice eligible duplicates for EXP and unlock permanent cosmetic rewards. Protected Base Cards, locked cards, Showcase cards, Stockroom cards, and other ineligible cards are excluded.

Sacrificing is permanent and grants Constellation EXP—not Stardust. Permanent progress stays recorded as new Chapters are added; special Astral tracks may appear during selected events.

🏪

Shop & Stockroom

Use fstock to prepare cards intended for sale and fshop to browse or manage listings. The Stockroom holds up to 100 cards and protects them from incompatible actions.

Unsold listings can return to the Stockroom. Review every character, rarity, quantity, and price before confirming a listing or purchase.

🛡️

Safe collecting

Use locks, tags, and the Stockroom to separate treasured cards from duplicates intended for burning, sacrifice, trading, or sale.

Never share your Discord token, password, recovery codes, or other credentials. Frog staff will never ask for them.

⚖️

Fair trading

Confirm all cards, items, quantities, and conditions before accepting. Reference prices are community guidelines, not guaranteed values, and real-money transactions involving Frog assets are not permitted.

🏕️

Guild participation

Joining Collector, Hoarder, or Ticket Master is optional. Contributions should never be used to pressure or shame members, and artificial progress through alternate accounts or exploits is prohibited.

🧪

Beta care

Features, rewards, prices, requirements, and available content may change. If you find a bug, stop using it and report it privately; honest accidental encounters are treated differently from exploitation.

Before a permanent action: trust the exact preview shown by Frog, then check the selected card, rarity, quantity, cost, and result one more time.
