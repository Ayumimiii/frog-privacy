Privacy,
without fog.

Frog uses the minimum information needed to recognize you, respond to commands, and preserve the progress you intentionally create.

Effective August 9, 2026

1. Information Frog uses

  • Your Discord user ID, username or display name, and avatar.
  • The content of messages that invoke Frog commands or provide replies needed to complete an interactive action.
  • Your Frog play progress, such as cards, inventory, currencies, cooldowns, settings, customizations, Guild participation, Shop activity, trades, and supported transaction histories.

2. Why it is used

Discord identifiers connect actions and saved progress to the correct player. Names and avatars help Frog display recognizable profiles, trade screens, leaderboards, and other social features. Command content tells Frog which action you requested. Progress records let your collection continue between sessions and help prevent duplicate rewards, abuse, or economy errors.

3. Message content

Frog processes messages when they are commands, supported prefixes, or required replies to an active interaction. Ordinary conversation is not treated as gameplay progress. Frog does not need your password, Discord token, recovery codes, private messages unrelated to Frog, microphone, camera, or precise location.

4. What may be visible

Features designed for community play may show limited information to other players, including your Discord identity, Frog profile, Showcase, Guild participation, Shop listings, and trade-related details. Frog does not sell your personal information.

5. Storage and protection

Progress is kept for as long as needed to operate Frog, preserve the economy, maintain security, and meet reasonable recordkeeping needs. Access should be limited to authorized maintainers and services needed to operate Frog. No online system can promise absolute security.

6. Your choices

You may stop using Frog at any time. To request access, correction, or deletion of your stored player data, contact Frog and Friends staff through the official support channel. Some transaction or moderation records may need to be retained or anonymized to protect other players and preserve economy integrity.

7. Changes and contact

This notice may change as Frog develops. Important changes will be communicated when reasonably possible. Questions or data requests should be sent through the official Frog and Friends support channel.
